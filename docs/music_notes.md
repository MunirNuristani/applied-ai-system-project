# Music notes (retrieval corpus)

Short, hand-written descriptions used as a second retrieval source next to
data/songs.csv. The explainer may only use facts from these notes and from the
retrieved song rows. Format: `## genre: <name>` or `## mood: <name>`.

## genre: ambient
Slow, textural music built from sustained sounds rather than hooks; usually low energy and often used as background for focus or sleep.

## genre: classical
Orchestral or solo-instrument compositions with wide dynamic range; typically highly acoustic and rarely tied to a single mood.

## genre: country
Story-driven songs built around guitar, steady rhythm and clear vocals; often warm and relaxed with a mid energy level.

## genre: edm
Electronic dance music made for clubs and festivals: steady four-on-the-floor pulse, high energy, low acousticness.

## genre: folk
Acoustic-led songs with an emphasis on lyrics and simple arrangements; gentle energy and high acousticness.

## genre: hip hop
Rhythm-forward music centered on rapped vocals over beats; energy ranges from laid back to aggressive.

## genre: indie pop
Pop songwriting with a lighter, more intimate production; usually mid energy with a bright or dreamy feel.

## genre: jazz
Improvisation-friendly music with swung rhythm and rich harmony; commonly relaxed to mid energy and fairly acoustic.

## genre: lofi
Soft, slightly dusty beats with mellow melodies, designed for studying and background listening; low energy and often acoustic-sounding.

## genre: pop
Hook-driven songs with polished production and broad appeal; mid to high energy and usually upbeat.

## genre: punk
Fast, short, loud songs with raw guitars; very high energy and low acousticness.

## genre: r&b
Smooth, groove-based vocal music with an emphasis on feel and melody; usually mid energy and romantic or mellow.

## genre: reggae
Off-beat guitar rhythms with a relaxed pulse and a sunny, easygoing feel; mid-low energy.

## genre: rock
Guitar, bass and drums driven songs with a strong backbeat; energy from mid to very high.

## genre: synthwave
Retro electronic music inspired by 1980s film and game soundtracks: analog synth sounds, driving tempos, often nostalgic or moody.

## mood: angry
Aggressive and confrontational; pairs with high energy and distorted or heavy sounds.

## mood: chill
Low-pressure and easygoing; background-friendly music at low to mid energy.

## mood: confident
Self-assured and bold; usually mid to high energy with a strong beat.

## mood: euphoric
Peak-moment, uplifting music; very high energy and bright, high-valence sound.

## mood: focused
Steady and unobtrusive music that keeps attention on a task; low to mid energy with few surprises.

## mood: happy
Bright and positive; high valence, upbeat tempo, mid to high energy.

## mood: intense
Driving and powerful; high energy and often fast tempo.

## mood: melancholy
Wistful or sad in tone; low valence and usually low to mid energy.

## mood: moody
Atmospheric and a little dark; mixed valence and mid energy.

## mood: nostalgic
Evokes the past; often retro production and warm, familiar melodies.

## mood: peaceful
Calm and spacious; very low energy and high acousticness.

## mood: relaxed
Unhurried and comfortable; low to mid energy.

## mood: romantic
Warm, intimate and melodic; typically mid-low energy with smooth vocals.
